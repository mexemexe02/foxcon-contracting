What We Do

Our Services

From ground-up builds to seasonal site maintenance, we cover every phase of your project with skilled teams and heavy equipment.

General Contracting

Full-service construction management for residential, commercial, and industrial projects. We handle permitting, subcontractor coordination, and on-site supervision from start to finish.

Excavation & Grading

Site preparation, trenching, land clearing, and precision grading for foundations, driveways, drainage systems, and landscaping — backed by a modern equipment fleet.

Demolition

Safe, controlled demolition of residential and commercial structures. Complete site cleanup and debris removal included — we leave your site ready for the next phase.

Snow Removal

Reliable winter services for commercial properties, parking lots, and residential complexes across Barrie. 24/7 storm response with plow trucks, loaders, and salting units.

Retaining Walls

Engineered retaining wall systems — segmental block, poured concrete, gabion, and timber. We design and build solutions for erosion control, terracing, and structural support.

Property Maintenance

Year-round property upkeep: lawn care, landscaping, asphalt repair, fence installation, pressure washing, and general site services for commercial and strata properties.

Who We Are

About Foxcon Contracting

Based in Barrie, Ontario, Foxcon Contracting has been serving Simcoe County for over 15 years. We're a family-operated general contractor built on the principles of hard work, craftsmanship, and safety.

Whether it's a custom home build, a commercial excavation, or emergency snow clearing, our team brings the same commitment to every job: show up on time, work safely, and deliver quality that lasts.

  • Licensed & fully insured — WSIB compliant
  • Serving Simcoe County and surrounding areas
  • Modern equipment fleet — excavators, loaders, dump trucks
  • Safety-certified crews — Working at Heights, WHMIS, First Aid
